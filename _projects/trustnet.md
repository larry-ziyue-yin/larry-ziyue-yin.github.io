TrustNet is a [Duke Kunshan University innovation project](https://ine.dukekunshan.edu.cn/project/trustnet/) exploring ways to identify misinformation in social media content. As the team's lead, I have worked on the idea of combining machine learning, social network analysis, and verification tools to help people evaluate what they see online.

The project focuses on the challenge of fake news in sports journalism and video content. The linked project description outlines the proposed approach; it is not a claim that every proposed component has been built.
