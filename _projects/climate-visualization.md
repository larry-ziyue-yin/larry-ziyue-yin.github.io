A group project for STATS 401 at Duke Kunshan University, built with Xin Jiang and Yitong Zhou. The site brings together climate indicators and wildfire observations in an interactive format.

Explore trends through coordinated charts, a global wildfire map, country comparisons, a correlation matrix, and a text visualization. Each view offers a different way to ask questions of the underlying data.
